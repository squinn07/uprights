REAL CROWD RECORDINGS
=====================

Without any files here the game synthesises its crowd. To use real stadium
recordings instead, save them in THIS folder with the names below, then
double-click  tools\pack-audio.bat  and reload index.html.

Each one is optional; the game uses whatever is present.

  crowd.wav     Looping bed: the steady buzz of a big crowd between kicks.
  stadium.wav   Looping bed: a loud stadium crowd. Rises while the ball is
                in the air and carries the roar on a make.
  cheer.wav     One-shot: played when a kick is good.
  boo.wav       One-shot: played when a kick misses.

(.mp3 and .ogg work too. WAV files are shrunk automatically: mono, 22 kHz,
at most 45 seconds, and the two beds are given a seamless loop.)


FREE RECORDINGS THAT FIT (Wikimedia Commons)
--------------------------------------------
Open each page, click "Download", and save it here under the name shown.

  stadium.wav  "Arrowhead Stadium crowd noise" - recorded at the Kansas City
               Chiefs' stadium (a real NFL crowd). 13.5 s.
               https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Arrowhead_Stadium_crowd_noise.wav
               Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0, by Kj1595 - credit required (see below).

  crowd.wav    "Large crowd, medium distance" - about a thousand people talking. 57 s.
               https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:360703_eguobyte_large-crowd-medium-distance-stereo.wav
               Licence: CC0 (public domain), by eguobyte.

  cheer.wav    "F1 race crowd applause and air horns" - a big outdoor crowd
               cheering. 28 s.
               https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:F1-Race-Crowd-Applause-Air-Horns.wav
               Licence: CC BY 4.0, by WebbFilmsUK - credit required (see below).

  boo.wav      "Audience booing". 5.5 s.
               https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Soundgoats_-_Audience_Booing.wav
               Licence: public domain.

The NFL's own broadcast audio is copyrighted and can't be used. Any other
recording you have the rights to works the same way.
